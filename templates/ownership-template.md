## Status definitions

- **Clear:** No known dependency prevents planned work.
- **At risk:** A dependency may miss its required date or contract.
- **Blocked:** Work cannot proceed until the named outcome is delivered.

Ledger entries must follow the ownership and dependency principle in [`memory/constitution.md`](/memory/constitution.md).
